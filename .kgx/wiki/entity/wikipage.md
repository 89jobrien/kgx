# WikiPage

Structured representation of a wiki page in [[WikiStore]].

## Fields

- `slug: String` — generated by [[slugify]]
- `category: WikiCategory` — [[WikiCategory]] (Summary, Entity, Topic)
- `title: String`
- `content: String` — full markdown body
- `summary: String`
